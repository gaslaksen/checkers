Support

Chaos Checkers — checkers where every jump earns you an ability.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?

Get in touch through the game's feedback page — tell us what happened and which device you were using, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Tap the ❔ in the top corner of the menu for the rules and every ability. In short: it's checkers, jumping is optional, and each piece you jump earns an ability you can use at the start of a later turn.

My online game won't connect

Both players need an internet connection. Some networks — often school, work or public Wi-Fi — block the direct connection the game uses. If a code won't connect, try mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network, and make sure your friend typed the code exactly.

My Game Center name doesn't show

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings, then open the game again. If you're not signed in, you'll appear as "You" or "Friend" instead.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the ⚙️ in the top corner of the menu and switch Sound off.

How do I get different boards?

Tap the two checkers above the title on the menu. After that, you can choose a board size and shape before each game.

Privacy Policy

Chaos Checkers · Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: Chaos Checkers has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your personal information, and we don't sell or share any.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your settings (such as whether sound is on) and simple results, like how many games you've played and won against each bot. This is saved only on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app — or, on the website, clearing the site's data — removes it.

Game Center

On iPhone, the game can use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, the game reads your Game Center name and picture from Apple so it can show them on the versus screen at the start of an online game, and sends them to the other player's device for the same reason. We don't store them. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Playing online with a friend

When you play with a friend using a code, your two devices connect directly to each other, and the moves pass between them. To introduce the two devices, the game uses a free public connection service, PeerJS, which needs your device's network (IP) address to make the connection. We don't receive or keep that address. Games against the bot, and games on one device, don't connect to anything.

Children

Chaos Checkers is for everyone, including children. Because the game doesn't collect personal information, there's nothing to collect from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

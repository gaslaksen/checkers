Privacy Policy

Chaos Checkers · Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version: Chaos Checkers has no accounts and no analytics, and we don't collect your personal information. The iPhone app shows ads from Google, which collects some information about your device to show them, as explained below. The website has no ads.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your settings (such as whether sound is on) and simple results, like how many games you've played and won against each bot. This is saved only on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app — or, on the website, clearing the site's data — removes it.

Game Center

On iPhone, the game can use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, the game reads your Game Center name and picture from Apple so it can show them on the versus screen at the start of an online game, and sends them to the other player's device for the same reason. We don't store them. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Ads in the iPhone app

The free iPhone app shows ads from Google AdMob, now and then between games, and when you choose to watch one (for example, to reroll your power-ups). There are no ads in your first few games. To choose and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google collects information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location and how you interact with the ads. How Google uses information from apps that use its services explains more.

Before ads use your device's advertising identifier, Apple's "Allow tracking?" question asks your permission. You can change your answer any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads still show if you say no, but they can't use that identifier. Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent form asks you first. You can change your choices in the game's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between games. Ads you choose to watch stay available. The purchase is handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details.

Playing online with a friend

When you play with a friend using a code, your two devices connect directly to each other, and the moves pass between them. To introduce the two devices, the game uses a free public connection service, PeerJS, which needs your device's network (IP) address to make the connection. We don't receive or keep that address. Games against the bot, and games on one device, don't connect to anything.

Children

Chaos Checkers is for everyone, including children. We don't collect personal information from anyone. The ads in the iPhone app are limited to ones rated suitable for general audiences.
